## Quantum-Cells (CELL)
Post-Quantum Cryptocurrency | GPU Mining | ML-DSA-87 | Proof-of-Entanglement

## Overview

Quantum-Cells (CELL) is an experimental next-generation cryptocurrency project focused on post-quantum cryptography, GPU-powered mining, and an alternative blockchain consensus architecture.

The project is designed around the idea that blockchain infrastructure must evolve beyond conventional cryptographic assumptions to remain secure in an era of increasingly powerful computing technologies, including the potential development of practical quantum computers.

Quantum-Cells combines post-quantum digital signatures, a GPU mining ecosystem, and an original consensus-related mechanism called Proof-of-Entanglement (PoE).

Its primary objective is to establish an open cryptocurrency network where participants can contribute computational resources through GPU mining while benefiting from cryptographic technologies designed to resist known quantum-computing attacks..

## Core Technology
1. Post-Quantum Cryptography

One of the fundamental components of Quantum-Cells is its approach to cryptographic security.

The project uses ML-DSA-87, standardized by NIST as FIPS 204, for digital transaction signatures.

ML-DSA is based on lattice-based cryptography and is designed to provide security against attacks from both classical and quantum computers, under its established security assumptions.

Key characteristics:

NIST-standardized post-quantum digital signatures.
Lattice-based cryptographic construction.
Designed to resist known quantum attacks against conventional public-key signature schemes.
Suitable for a future-oriented blockchain security architecture.

By incorporating ML-DSA-87, Quantum-Cells aims to reduce dependence on traditional digital signature systems that may be vulnerable to sufficiently capable quantum computers.

Note: Post-quantum cryptography does not guarantee absolute security. The implementation and its surrounding infrastructure still require independent review and security auditing.

2. Proof-of-Entanglement (PoE)

Quantum-Cells introduces an original mechanism called Proof-of-Entanglement.

The concept connects newly submitted transactions with previously confirmed blockchain history.

Under the described mechanism, transactions reference between 2 and 10 previously confirmed transactions, creating additional relationships within the transaction history.

The intended objectives include:

Strengthening relationships between confirmed transactions.
Creating additional transaction-history dependencies.
Supporting the network's validation architecture.
Providing an alternative approach to organizing transaction confirmation.

Proof-of-Entanglement is a project-specific consensus-related mechanism. It should not be confused with physical quantum entanglement or interpreted as a claim that the blockchain performs quantum computations.

3. GPU Mining

Quantum-Cells provides a GPU mining ecosystem based on NVIDIA graphics hardware.

Mining allows network participants to contribute computational resources and receive CELL rewards according to the network's mining rules.

The project is designed to support GPU-based participation through its dedicated mining software, Quantum-Cells Hive (currently distributed under the QSDM Hive name).

Mining infrastructure is intended to provide:

Mining Windows and Rewards

Mining operates in scheduled 7-day windows with a predefined reward budget.

Rewards are distributed proportionally according to accepted mining work.

The system also supports deferred mining bonds, subject to network rules.

Nodes with an unpaid deferred bond initially operate at 10% reward weight until the required 10 CELL bond is fully paid.

Mining enrollment and active mining windows may depend on the current network status.
